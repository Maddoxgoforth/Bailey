# VSL Script — Bailey's Inner Circle

For the `VideoSlot` in `src/components/sections/Hero.tsx`. Written to run
~2:15 at a natural pace (under the 3-minute target with room to spare).
Landscape 16:9, Bailey talking straight to camera.

Broad hook first (works on anyone who's ever wanted access to a creator
they follow), then narrows to Bailey specifically, then the two things her
audience actually wants: weekly personal access to her, and her recipes.

---

**[0:00–0:12] — THE HOOK (broad)**

> If you've ever followed someone online, watched every single thing they
> post, and thought — *"I wish I could just actually talk to this
> person"* — stay with me for a second. Because I built exactly that.

**[0:12–0:28] — WHO THIS IS**

> Hey, I'm Bailey. If you've been around my stuff for a while, you already
> know two things about me — I love talking to you guys, and I love
> cooking. So I built one thing that gives you both.

**[0:28–1:00] — THE OFFER: WEEKLY ACCESS (lead benefit)**

> It's called Bailey's Inner Circle. Every single week, I hop on live
> inside the community and we just talk. Not a script, not a set topic —
> whatever's going on with you, what I'm up to, life, whatever. And yeah,
> if you've got a recipe question, ask me that too. It's a real
> conversation, not a comment I'll never see.

**[1:00–1:30] — THE SECOND BENEFIT: THE RECIPES**

> On top of that, you get all 100+ of my personal recipes — the actual
> ones I cook in my own kitchen, not something copy-pasted off the
> internet. Broken down simple enough that anyone can follow them, and
> I'm adding new ones all the time.

**[1:30–1:50] — COMMUNITY / PROOF IT'S REAL**

> And inside, it's not just me posting into a void either. You can post,
> ask me things directly, and actually get a reply from me — not a bot,
> not an assistant. Me.

**[1:50–2:05] — PRICE / VALUE**

> All of that — weekly access to me, 100+ recipes, a real community — is
> $5 a month. Less than your coffee order.

**[2:05–2:15] — CTA**

> So if you've ever wanted to actually be in my world instead of just
> watching it from the outside — this is it. Hit the button below and
> join the Inner Circle. I'll see you in there this week.

---

## Notes for filming

- Talk straight to camera the whole time — no B-roll needed to keep this
  simple; a few recipe/cooking cutaways over the 1:00–1:30 section are
  optional, not required.
- Once filmed and hosted (Wistia/YouTube/Loom), pass the embed URL to
  `VideoSlot` in `Hero.tsx` via its `embedUrl` prop — no other changes
  needed.
